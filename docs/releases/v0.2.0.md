# nf-genome_to_diffraction v0.2.0

Release date: 2026-08-23

## Release type

This is an experimental research-prototype release for bounded two-component
molecular replacement. It supports exactly `ASU = nA + mB`: obtain and review
one A state, fix it, then search explicit positive B copy counts.

It is not a production release, an automated protein-identity claim, a complete
assembly-inference system, or an M6 scientifically validated release.

## Included

- fixed-A/joint-B Phaser with retained command, PDB/MTZ, packing, placement,
  B-specific TFZ, and incremental LLG evidence;
- reviewed-parent and normal-workflow 6RTZ `1A + 1B` execution;
- explicit joint copy counts, qualified with 3U7Q `2A + 2B`;
- deterministic full-catalogue B planning with a fixed first-wave cap of 25,
  neutral missing gel evidence, and typed deferred/unsearchable rows;
- one terminal result per selected candidate and a complete attempt summary;
- missing-B, wrong-B, first-copy-route non-regression, and three-component
  abstention controls; and
- the reproducible Pixi/Nextflow environment plus reviewed Marmic execution
  wrapper retained from v0.1.

## Control evidence

- 6RTZ `1A + 1B`: one packed HisF parent and one packed HisH placement, partner
  TFZ 71.2 and incremental LLG 5054.654.
- 3U7Q `2A + 2B`: two packed A copies and two packed B copies, partner TFZ
  371.4 and incremental LLG 229112.711.
- Full Thermotoga catalogue: 1,846 proteins imported as 1,846 exact sequence
  groups; 1,845 non-A candidates retained; exactly HisH selected and searched;
  1,844 candidates retained with typed unsearchable reasons.
- P6 terminal Marmic evidence: retained run
  `gtd-heteromer-smoke-20260823T140153Z-24f733ca75f5-4ab869b5`, Slurm `633156`,
  from source `24f733ca75f54e5d8bff511a7417e9daa8244d54`; all 47 final
  checksums, six composition assessments, and seven Phenix executable
  identities verified after corrected bounded collection.

The P6-v2 gate binds every control to its source preparation, parent solution,
catalogue universe, model, MTZ, and retained checksum. A deliberately wrong B
placement remains `search_evidence_only` even when Phaser packs it and reports
incremental LLG 327.049 and TFZ 5.1 in the fallback cohort; it cannot become a
complete-composition claim.

Packing, LLG, TFZ, model quality, Matthews probability, and gel compatibility
remain evidence fields. They do not by themselves prove biological identity,
stoichiometry, or a complete asymmetric-unit composition.

## Known limitations

- Exactly two protein components are supported. 9ECN is reported as
  `unsupported_component_count`; valid partial A+B evidence may be retained,
  but no three-component reconstruction or complete-composition claim is made.
- Candidate execution depends on an available checksum-bound MR model. Missing
  models remain typed unsearchable candidates rather than inferred absences.
- The wrong-B control protects the no-claim boundary; an MR placement is search
  evidence and does not establish partner identity.
- Membrane/exported localisation filtering, unknown operator crystals, AF3
  complex modelling, broader assembly inference, and local ESM Atlas are
  deferred.
- M6 scientific acceptance remains held. M6 orchestration evidence does not
  validate the heteromer workflow.
- Crystallographic-context propagation, Free-R membership identity, parent
  uncertainty/support semantics, transactional output handling, final
  Rwork/Rfree parsing, and sequence-map failure classification remain
  incomplete.
- Some raw-input/cache invalidation, network-site, retry, packaging, and full
  M6 measurement hardening remains deferred.

## Compatibility and migration

The immutable `v0.1.0` tag remains the archival single-component baseline.
v0.2 adds new heteromer records and execution paths without rewriting v0.1
evidence. Existing single-component stages remain available. Operators must
explicitly select the heteromer analysis stage and provide the normal reviewed
A checkpoint; the pipeline does not infer permission to search assemblies.

## Reproducibility

The release is a checksummed source/tag research release consisting of the Git
tag, source tree, `pixi.lock`, tracked schemas, examples, changelog, release
notes, and immutable control evidence. It does not claim a separately qualified
binary or wheel distribution.

Phenix 2.1-6048 is licensed external software and is not redistributed. The
accepted control runs verify SHA-256 identities for all seven required Phenix
executables.
