# v0.3 candidate-confidence-first roadmap

## Authority and status

This document defines the canonical execution order for the unreleased
`main` line after immutable release `v0.2.0`. Detailed Phase III method, finding, validation, and release
documents remain applicable, but they may not advance work ahead of the order
defined here.

The current published research release remains experimental `v0.2.0`.
`v0.3.0` is not complete and must not be represented as validated until every
release condition in this document is satisfied.

Current critical-path state on 2026-09-02: Marmic job `637462` completed all
75 unique one-copy MR hypotheses and cached all 75 on resume, but the wrapper
mistook two failed first-attempt retry rows for extra hypotheses and failed its
post-resume identity check. No candidate output or cache from that failed run is
accepted. Retry-aware qualification and finaliser-level failed-child collection
are under local repair; they require the complete local gate, an exact pushed
`main` commit, green CI, and one fresh source-bound discovery/screen chain before
the A-seed review in step 6 can begin.

## Goal

First produce the strongest scientifically defensible candidate assessment for
each of the three frozen unknown crystals:

- `AD4QS1P4G2_18`;
- `CD4QS2P2G1_15`; and
- `CD6QS2P2G1_5`.

The assessment must preserve all alternatives and may end at an exact sequence,
a sequence-equivalence group, a protein family, an unresolved shortlist, or an
explicit no-supported-candidate result. It must never force an identity.

Only after all three candidate assessments and their supervisor-ready reports
are complete may development advance through the remaining M6, finding-closure,
unknown-pass-2, final-report, atlas, packaging, and release work required for
validated research version `v0.3.0`.

## Scientific execution policy

### Initial screen

- Matthews enumeration retains the complete configured expected-copy range.
- Full catalogue sequence mass, not trimmed search-model mass, determines each
  expected copy hypothesis.
- The initial MR task searches exactly one copy, regardless of expected ASU
  copy count.
- No special four-copy filter, joint-copy execution mode, or hidden copy-count
  upper bound may remove an otherwise retained expected-copy hypothesis.
- The pass-1 execution budget remains at most 25 ranked hypotheses per crystal.
  The frozen current inputs produce at least 25 admissible hypotheses for each
  crystal under the one-copy policy, so the qualified screen must emit 75
  independent MR tasks.
- Slurm owns aggregate task admission. Nextflow may apply no global, profile,
  queue-size, or process-local concurrency ceiling to these scientific tasks.

### Resource policy

Each hypothesis receives a content-addressed resource plan derived before
execution from reflection count, moving-model polymer atoms, the one searched
copy, any fixed-component atoms, and crystallographic symmetry multiplicity.
First attempts use the established 4/6/8-CPU tiers. One classified
resource/infrastructure retry may scale to at most 16 CPUs, 64 GB, and 48 hours.
Scientific no-hit, malformed evidence, and contract failure are not retryable
resource events.

### Sequential copy completion

Only an explicitly approved one-copy A seed may continue. Additional copies are
placed one at a time toward the retained Matthews expectation. Every supported
and unsupported intermediate state is retained. The workflow must not replace
this evidence chain with a simultaneous high-copy search.

### Confidence boundary

LLG, TFZ, and packing support candidate screening but do not establish
identity. A high-confidence assignment requires coherent evidence from:

1. a valid, reproducible MR placement;
2. physically and crystallographically credible packing;
3. sequential copy behaviour compatible with retained alternatives;
4. stable refinement using the original Free-R-bearing diffraction data;
5. interpretable maps and model-versus-data agreement;
6. sequence-discriminating evidence against the complete trusted catalogue;
7. explicit human review; and
8. complete provenance and checksums.

If those layers disagree or cannot discriminate candidates, the final status
must remain uncertain.

## Candidate-confidence critical path

### 1. Freeze and classify the failed predecessor

The terminal predecessor screen and every failed or cancelled predecessor are
diagnostic only. Their scientific caches are non-reusable. Retain their bounded
logs, collected controller evidence, exact failure signatures, and child-task
inventory without promoting any hit/no-hit or identity claim.

Acceptance evidence:

- scheduler, exit, source, parent, provider, input, and checksum ownership;
- exact submitted/completed/killed/unemitted task counts;
- explicit `scientific_evidence_accepted=false`; and
- no cache or result path reused by a successor.

### 2. Implement the one-copy initial screen

Remove the joint-copy option and the four-copy execution filter from the active
candidate funnel and workflow. Preserve Matthews expected copy count in the
hypothesis while setting the initial searched copy count to one. Build the
dynamic resource plan from the one-copy workload and advance the relevant
adapter/cache identity.

Focused acceptance cases must prove:

- expected copy counts greater than four remain eligible;
- the searched copy count is one for every initial hypothesis;
- each frozen crystal emits 25 hypotheses from the checksum-frozen audit
  inputs;
- all 75 task identities are unique and stable under input permutation;
- resource plans remain separate from scientific hypothesis identity;
- a resource-plan mutation changes only execution/cache evidence; and
- no active source or configuration contains the retired four-copy/joint-copy
  admission policy.

### 3. Preserve child evidence on failure

Extend strict failed-run collection so every submitted MR child has one bounded,
checksum-authenticated diagnostic record even if the controller terminates
before the normal success inventory is published.

The record must include, when available:

- crystal, hypothesis, model, sequence group, expected copies, and attempted
  copy number;
- native scheduler ID and terminal scheduler/exit state;
- allocated CPUs, memory, time, attempt, elapsed time, and observed resources;
- command, Phaser log, normalised result, solution assets, and checksums; and
- an explicit distinction between completed scientific evidence, killed work,
  absent output, and unsubmitted work.

Collection must be read-only, bounded, path-confined, content-inventoried, and
incapable of making a failed cache reusable.

### 4. Qualify one exact source

Run focused tests first, followed by the named locked integration gate. The
exact pushed commit must pass CI. Deploy only that pushed source and its reviewed
wrapper scripts. Build a fresh source-bound private execution authority whenever
scientific source semantics change.

Required evidence:

- formatting, lint, type, docs, schema, package, and diff hygiene;
- focused ranking, resource, retry, collection, and non-stub Nextflow tests;
- the complete local integration/release gate;
- exact commit/tree and green CI run/job; and
- deployed-tool hashes and strict Phenix/runtime bindings.

### 5. Run fresh discovery and pass-1 screening

Stage and submit one owned discovery run, then one parent-bound screen from the
same exact source. Never use failed, cancelled, predecessor-scientific, or
cross-source caches.

Screen acceptance requires:

- all 75 independent one-copy hypotheses emitted without executor throttling;
- complete per-crystal candidate, task, outcome, resource, retry, and cache
  inventories;
- exact provider/model/input/runtime provenance;
- first and resume traces with justified cache reuse;
- checksum-reconstructible outputs; and
- no reinterpretation of execution failure as scientific no-hit.

### 6. Stop for A-seed review

On successful collection, publish one owned A review package per proceeding
crystal and stop. Each package must retain every candidate, result, asset,
advisory, and rejection reason. No decision may be inferred from rank.

The user must explicitly approve, reject, or defer each candidate target.

### 7. Complete approved copies and finalists

For each approved seed:

1. place additional copies sequentially;
2. retain the best supported copy state and every failed/unsupported addition;
3. refine only the bounded retained finalists;
4. validate Free-R identity and preservation;
5. retain coordinates, maps, raw logs, Rwork/Rfree, geometry and warnings; and
6. compare leading candidates and credible alternatives on the same evidence
   basis.

### 8. Resolve sequence support and report candidates

Score map-supported sequence evidence against the complete trusted catalogue.
Preserve equivalent sequences and compatible loci rather than selecting one by
annotation preference. Stop for mandatory sequence review.

For each crystal, publish a private supervisor-ready report containing:

- leading candidate and complete retained alternatives;
- gene/protein identifiers and coordinate provenance;
- sequence identity/coverage and model limitations;
- expected versus supported copy count;
- LLG, TFZ, packing, refinement, map and sequence evidence;
- negative evidence and explicit rejection reasons;
- confidence class with stated limitations; and
- exact source, tool, database, input, decision and checksum provenance.

### Candidate-roadmap completion condition

This first objective is complete only when every frozen crystal has one
checksum-reconstructible terminal candidate assessment and all required A and
sequence decisions are recorded. An honest unresolved endpoint counts as
completion; a guessed identity does not.

## Remaining v0.3 development after candidate assessment

### M6 operational and leakage qualification

- qualify the operational track on the exact release line;
- produce an observed checksum-bound cache-mutation record;
- run the separately owned leakage track from the accepted operational parent;
- verify truth isolation, task/cache inventories, resources and outputs; and
- close the cancellation and failed-collection defects.

### Complete finding closure

Finish `RG0` through `RG7`. Every original and new finding must have a final
`Fixed`, `Superseded`, or `Deleted` disposition backed by its regression,
exact-source CI, and required fixed-HPC evidence. Unknown pass 2 must reject an
absent, stale, changed, or incomplete closure package.

### Unknown pass 2 and composition analysis

- continue credible A states through the bounded B-F beam;
- validate depth three with 9ECN;
- keep depths four through six explicitly provisional;
- limit attempts to 25 per depth and 100 additional-component attempts per
  crystal;
- use the retained no-A expansion only for a complete pass-1 zero-pack state;
- stop at every mandatory composition or sequence review; and
- never tune thresholds from unknown outcomes.

### Final reports, atlas and package surface

- complete per-crystal and cross-crystal reports;
- keep private scientific reports outside the public atlas;
- expose only `genome-to-diffraction` from the installed research package;
- keep the HPC client as an internal Pixi/zipapp validation tool;
- finish canonical module/file naming and remove operational milestone names
  from active executable code;
- make current contracts canonical and isolate release-rotated history; and
- generate deterministic offline HTML and frozen release snapshots from tracked
  sources.

### v0.3.0 release condition

Release only when:

- all three candidate analyses and both unknown passes are terminal;
- all required human decisions are recorded;
- M6 operational, mutation, and leakage evidence passes;
- all findings have final dispositions;
- 9ECN accepts depth three and deeper support remains provisional;
- package, schema, documentation, atlas and install parity passes;
- the exact release commit passes full local, CI, and fixed-HPC gates; and
- the release tag, notes, evidence indexes, and snapshots are immutable and
  mutually checksum-consistent.

## Operational rules

- The active pointer is the sole mutable owner of integrated-run state.
- Never infer ownership from recency or select another run automatically.
- Use the reviewed wrapper only; no raw SSH or direct scheduler commands.
- Issue one shell command per tool call.
- Never submit a duplicate job or reuse a failed/cancelled scientific cache.
- Preserve user work and private data.
- Do not journal unchanged monitoring state.
- Do not fabricate human approval.
- Pause and request the exact decision at every mandatory review boundary.
