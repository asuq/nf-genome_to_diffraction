Documentation / Workflow stage
Stage 06

Bounded composition expansion

After reviewing the first component, evaluate candidate gene products from the supplied list as possible heteromer partners. Each hypothesis adds one candidate gene product to the retained component set.

Current maturity: scientifically validated to depth three

Visible limitation. Depths 4-6 remain visible but unvalidated and cannot support a complete-composition claim.

How the heteromer-partner loop works

A gene product is the protein encoded by one gene. Each hypothesis adds one candidate gene product to the retained component set and tests whether they can work together as a heteromer. Competing hypotheses may test different candidate partners.

Component 1→Component 2→Component 3→Component 4→Component 5→Component 6
Evaluate one candidate gene product as a possible heteromer partner→Test 1, 2, 3, or 4 copies with Molecular Replacement→Collect states→Human review
Stop → publish the reviewed result   |   Continue ↻ repeat with the next partner candidate.

Limits: at most 25 attempts per depth, up to three retained parent states, 100 partner attempts per crystal, and six total components. Depths four through six remain provisional.

Purpose

Test whether a candidate gene product can work with the retained component or components as a heteromer, without selecting one partner in advance or treating search exhaustion as proof that the composition is complete.

Inputs

  • Reviewed first-component structure or another approved retained state
  • Candidate gene products and their eligible structure models
  • Parent-bound expansion plan and reviewer composition decisions

Outputs

  • Per-depth attempt inventories and retained parent states
  • Component scope decisions
  • Claim-free terminal composition and sequence review packages

Decisions and statuses

  • Retain at most three parent states before each expansion depth
  • Exclude gene products already represented in the retained state
  • Create competing hypotheses that each add one candidate gene product
  • For each candidate partner, test physically possible copy counts from 1 through 4
  • Rank hypotheses by localisation, molecular-weight evidence, total-composition Matthews plausibility, model quality, and structural diversity
  • Bound work to 25 attempts per depth and 100 partner attempts per crystal
  • Stop on depth, budget, no-model, review hold, or no residual content

Claim and failure boundaries

  • The workflow does not manually choose one partner before testing
  • One hypothesis adds one candidate gene product to its retained parent state
  • Depth three is positively qualified by 9ECN
  • Depths four through six are always provisional
  • No automatic identity or complete-composition claim is made between depths
Run commands and implementation links

Use commands only with the reviewed configuration, immutable source, and owned inputs for the intended site. Placeholders are deliberate.
