Documentation / Workflow stage
Stage 06

Bounded composition expansion

After reviewing the first component, test as many as five additional distinct proteins from the supplied list, giving at most six components in total.

Current maturity: scientifically validated to depth three

Visible limitation. Depths 4-6 remain visible but unvalidated and cannot support a complete-composition claim.

How the additional distinct-component loop works

The first component is reviewed before expansion. The workflow may then add as many as five different components, one at each depth.

Component 1→Component 2→Component 3→Component 4→Component 5→Component 6
Choose another protein from the supplied list→Test 1, 2, 3, or 4 copies with Molecular Replacement→Collect states→Human review
Stop → publish the reviewed result   |   Continue ↻ repeat the cycle for the next component.

Limits: at most 25 attempts per depth, up to three retained parent states, 100 additional-component attempts per crystal, and six total components. Depths four through six remain provisional.

Purpose

Test whether another protein from the supplied list explains the remaining diffraction signal without treating search exhaustion as proof that the composition is complete.

Inputs

  • Reviewed first-component structure or an approved alternative search state
  • All-eligible model registry
  • Parent-bound depth plan and reviewer composition decisions

Outputs

  • Per-depth attempt inventories and retained parent states
  • Component scope decisions
  • Claim-free terminal composition and sequence review packages

Decisions and statuses

  • Retain at most three first-component states before expansion
  • For each additional component, test physically possible copy counts from 1 through 4
  • Bound work to 25 attempts per depth and 100 additional-component attempts per crystal
  • Stop on depth, budget, no-model, review hold, or no residual content

Claim and failure boundaries

  • Depth three is positively qualified by 9ECN
  • Depths four through six are always provisional
  • No automatic identity or complete-composition claim is made between depths
